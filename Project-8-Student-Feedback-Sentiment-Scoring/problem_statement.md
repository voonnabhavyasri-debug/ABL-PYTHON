# Problem Statement
Analyze a short student feedback comment using a small list of positive and negative words. Calculate a simple score and classify the comment as positive, negative, or neutral.

## Objectives
- Accept a feedback comment.
- Count known positive and negative words.
- Calculate a sentiment score.
- Display a basic classification.

## Assumptions
This is a simple word-list demonstration. It cannot understand context, sarcasm, or words outside the lists and should not be treated as a reliable measure of student feelings.

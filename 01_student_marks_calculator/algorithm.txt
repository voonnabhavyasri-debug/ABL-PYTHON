Algorithm: Student Marks and Average Calculator

1. Start.
2. Read the student's name.
3. Read marks for subject 1, subject 2, and subject 3.
4. Convert the marks to numeric values.
5. Calculate total = mark1 + mark2 + mark3.
6. Calculate average = total / 3.
7. Display the student's name, total, and average.
8. Stop.

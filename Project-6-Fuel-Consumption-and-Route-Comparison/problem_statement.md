# Problem Statement
Compare two travel routes by estimating fuel required and fuel cost using route distance, vehicle efficiency, and fuel price.

## Objectives
- Estimate litres required for each route.
- Calculate estimated fuel costs.
- Identify the route with lower fuel cost.

## Assumptions
Distances and fuel price are non-negative. Fuel efficiency is greater than zero in kilometres per litre. Only fuel cost is compared; tolls and travel time are excluded.

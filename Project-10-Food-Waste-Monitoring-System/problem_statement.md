# Problem Statement
A cafeteria can record how much food is prepared and how much remains unused. This program calculates total food prepared, total food wasted, and the waste percentage, then classifies the waste level.

## Objectives
- Record prepared and wasted quantities for multiple items.
- Calculate total quantities.
- Calculate waste percentage.
- Classify the waste level using a threshold.

## Assumptions
Quantities are measured in kilograms. Prepared and wasted values must be non-negative, and waste cannot exceed the amount prepared. Waste above 20% is classified as High; otherwise it is Within Target.

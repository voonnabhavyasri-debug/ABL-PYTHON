PROJECT 1: PERSONAL PORTFOLIO WEBSITE — TEST CASES

TC01 — Open webpage
Steps: Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
Expected: The portfolio page loads and styling is visible.

TC02 — Navigation
Steps: Click About, Education, Skills, Projects, and Contact.
Expected: The page scrolls to the matching section.

TC03 — Home content
Steps: View the Home section.
Expected: Name placeholder, role, introduction, and Contact Me button appear.

TC04 — Education
Steps: Scroll to Education.
Expected: Two education cards appear with sample fields.

TC05 — Skills
Steps: Scroll to Technical Skills.
Expected: Four skill labels and progress bars appear.

TC06 — Projects
Steps: Scroll to My Projects.
Expected: Three project cards and tags appear.

TC07 — Contact button
Steps: Click Contact Me in the hero section.
Expected: The Contact section appears.

TC08 — Email link
Steps: Click the sample email address.
Expected: The default email app may open, depending on system settings.

TC09 — Hover effects
Steps: Hover over the Contact Me button and project cards.
Expected: A visual hover effect appears.

TC10 — Responsive layout
Steps: Resize the browser to a narrow/mobile width.
Expected: Cards stack in one column and navigation links wrap.

TC11 — Back to top
Steps: Click Back to top in the footer.
Expected: The page scrolls to Home.

TC12 — CSS connection
Steps: Temporarily rename style.css and refresh, then restore its original name.
Expected: Styling disappears when CSS is missing and returns when restored.

Record PASS or FAIL only after performing each test.
